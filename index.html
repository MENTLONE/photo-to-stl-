The preview is a 2D heightmap representation. STL exports a closed 3D mesh in millimeters.

What this does: Converts brightness into surface height. It creates a relief, not a full 3D reconstruction of hidden sides. Great for lithophanes, portraits, plaques and decorative panels. Your photo is processed locally; nothing is uploaded. STL generation may take a moment at high resolution.
